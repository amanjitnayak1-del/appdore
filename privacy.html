Privacy Policy

DoreBox ("we", "our", or "us") operates this content gateway page. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit this page, we may collect:

  • Log data — IP address, browser type, referring URL, date and time of visit.
  • URL parameters — subjectid and detailpath values passed in the URL to route you to the correct content. These are not stored on our servers.
  • Ad network data — Third-party advertising networks (such as Adsterra) may set cookies and collect data as described in their own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display the correct content link after the timer completes.
  • To improve page performance and user experience.
  • To display relevant advertisements that help keep DoreBox free.

3. Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party advertising services including Adsterra. These services may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve ads based on your interests. We do not control the data collected by these third parties. You can opt out of personalised advertising via youronlinechoices.com.

4. Cookies

We do not set first-party cookies. Third-party ad scripts may set their own cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; this may affect ad display but will not affect content access.

5. Deep Links

After the timer completes, clicking "Continue to App" opens the DoreBox mobile application via a dorebox:// deep link. No personal data is transmitted through this link beyond the content identifiers already present in the URL you arrived with.

6. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Retention

Server logs are retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes, then automatically deleted.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold. Contact us at the address below to exercise these rights.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the page after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email: privacy@dorebox.app
